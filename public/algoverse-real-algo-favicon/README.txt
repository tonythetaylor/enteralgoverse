ALGOVERSE / ALGO original-character favicon

All icon imagery is cropped directly from your uploaded algo-sheet.webp without regenerating the character.
Copy favicon.ico, favicon.svg, favicon-32.png, apple-touch-icon.png, icon-192.png, icon-512.png into your Vite public/ directory.

Suggested HTML head:
<link rel="icon" href="/favicon.svg?v=3" type="image/svg+xml" />
<link rel="alternate icon" href="/favicon.ico?v=3" sizes="any" />
<link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="/apple-touch-icon.png?v=3" />

If old favicon shows, hard refresh, close/reopen the tab, or use v=4.
